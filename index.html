Ludo King Quest Rules

🏆 20 Levels Quest: Each level consists of 10 matches (total 200 matches across 20 levels).
✔ Passing Rule (8/10 Wins): You must win at least 8 out of 10 matches in a level to advance. Completing each level grants 20 BDT directly to your wallet!
✖ Hardcore Failure Rule (3 Losses): If you lose 3 matches in any level, your run is broken and you reset back to Level 1! (You can watch a Rewarded Video Ad to revive 1 loss).
💰 Cash Withdrawal: Minimum withdrawal threshold is 200 BDT (clearing 10 levels). Withdraw directly via bKash, Nagad, or Rocket.
🤖 AI Difficulty Scaling: Levels 1-2: Easy | Levels 3-4: Medium | Levels 5-6: Hard | Levels 7-8: Extreme | Levels 9-20: Never-Won (Rigged).
